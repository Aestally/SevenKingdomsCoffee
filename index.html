BRAND STORY · SEVEN KINGDOMS

Coffee has always traveled across kingdoms.

From the highlands where beans are grown, to the ports where they are shipped, to the roasteries where aroma is awakened, and finally to the cup that begins someone's day — every cup passes through many lands. We call them the Seven Kingdoms: a symbol of the seven stages that shape every coffee.

01

Origin

Every bean begins with soil, altitude, rain and sun.

02

Selection

Only the right lots are chosen for flavour, consistency and character.

03

Roasting

Heat turns green beans into aroma, body and balance.

04

Grinding

Precision turns roast into texture, extraction and clarity.

05

Brewing

Water meets coffee, and flavour becomes experience.

06

Sharing

A cup is rarely enjoyed alone; it connects people.

07

Return

Every sip inspires the next harvest, roast and journey.

From origin to cup, every kingdom counts.
